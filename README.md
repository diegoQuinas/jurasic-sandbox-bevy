
# Dinosaur simulator

Dead simple dinosaur simulator made 100% in rust using Bevy ECS minimal.

![Example gameplay](./assets/dino-sim.png)

## Instructions

To play just use your keys to move the camera.

You can also press middle mouse button and drag.

To see the charts press `1`, `2`, or `3`, you can also click on the tui.
